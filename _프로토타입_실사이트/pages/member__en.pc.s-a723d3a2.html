What Is Self-Referral in Crypto?

Self-referral is the practice of registering multiple exchange accounts under different identities — then using your own referral code to claim the cashback commission that would normally go to an external referrer. At surface level, it seems like a clever workaround. In practice, it creates three serious problems: exchanges actively flag multi-account behavior, permanent account suspension is a real and documented outcome, and the operators running these schemes can disappear without notice.
