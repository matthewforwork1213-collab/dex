> This document is the reference document (no merged version, organized 2026-10-01).
> **Scope**: user-screen-only requirements (FR) + system logic (SYS) + notifications (NTF). SYS and NTF are common logic without screens, so they are **duplicated with the same content in both the User and Back Office documents**. Back-office-only requirements are in `분할문서/백오피스/요구사항정의서_en.md`.
> **ID rule**: REQ numbers keep the merged numbering from before the split (skipped numbers are requirements of the other part).
> **Type**: FR = user screen (front-end), AD = admin (back office), SYS = system logic (batch, judgment, payout), NTF = notification.
> All period and date judgments are **based on UTC**, and rewards are in **USDT**. Dates and times on the user screen are **UTC values converted to and shown in the user device's local time** (NFR-011).
> **Implementation basis (confirmed 2026-10-12)**: the **screen layout** of the mockup default screen (V2, event-style design) + the **rules** in this document. V2 changes only the layout (design); for copy and policy the documents prevail. Parts of the mockup that differ from the document rules (secondary text at the top, English "payback" wording, schedule expanded by default, etc.) are not implemented.
> REQ numbers marked **"(see the back-office Requirements Definition)"** are not in this document; they are in `분할문서/백오피스/요구사항정의서_en.md`.

**Terms (confirmed 2026-10-12)**

| Term | Definition |
|------|------|
| Achieved | The state of having completed a line (Bingo Reward), completed the Full Board (Full Board Reward) or placed within the ranking. The reward is **only expected to be received**; no money has been paid yet. Reward amounts in My Rewards on the user screen (badges "Pending" / "Calculating" / "Confirmed") are amounts based on what has been achieved |
| Paid (credited) | Actually crediting the rewards confirmed at the start of the payout period to the amount payable to the participating UID. Rewards are paid only once, at the start of the payout period, based on the final state. The user screen says "paid" (지급) and the system process is called "credit" (적립); they mean the same |
| Losing the participating UID | Users cannot unlink a UID themselves. A participating UID is cut off for two reasons only: **ownership transfer** (someone else takes it through ownership verification) and **issue UID designation** (withdrawal is a separate status). The judgment time is the time in the TetherMax account DB history |
| Reset (`RESET`) | The participating UID is lost during the participation period. The user becomes **like a first-time visitor** on that board (checked cells, lines, Full Board, ranking and achieved rewards all disappear). A new UID can be selected, and the new UID starts with its own gauge. Reason `OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER` / `ISSUE_UID` |
| Forfeited (`FORFEITED`) | The participating UID is lost after aggregation starts and before payout. No rewards; if the user completed the Full Board, the ranking place is left vacant. Reason `OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER` / `ISSUE_UID` |
| UID change after payout | Ownership transfer or issue UID designation after payment (crediting). Amounts already paid are not clawed back; the original TUID's participation status and paid amounts are not changed, and only the history is recorded |

### 1.4 Gauge · Cells

| ID | Type | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria |
|----|------|----------|----------|-----------|
| REQ-034 | SYS | The gauge reflects only cashback from trading. | Must | Other sources such as Event Rewards are excluded |
| REQ-035 | SYS | Once the participation period starts, the gauge of eligible UIDs accumulates automatically. Non-member UIDs are also recorded. | Must | When the participating UID is registered, the gauge accumulated since the participation start date is already reflected (retroactive) |
| REQ-036 | SYS | The gauge reflects only cashback brought in by **Bingo Batches** run during the participation period (including the last day). The Bingo Batch is a MAX BOARD-only daily batch that reads the results of the cashback batch (the existing per-exchange daily batch); once a day, at a fixed time common to all exchanges (a setting, after the Bitget cashback batch, exact time undecided #BATCH), it updates the gauge, streak, streak cell activation and ranking together. | Must | Cashback arriving after the last day's Bingo Batch is not reflected. Not reflected if the last day's Bingo Batch is delayed and runs after 00:00:00 UTC of the next day, or if the last day's cashback batch finishes after that day's Bingo Batch and slips to the next day (handled by CS). On other dates, a cashback batch that finished late is recorded by the next day's Bingo Batch under the original cashback batch date |
| REQ-037 | SYS | A gauge already filled is not clawed back. | Must | No gauge decrease |
| REQ-038 | FR | The user chooses which cell to check. | Must | No recommendation or highlight guidance |
| REQ-039 | FR | An amount cell can be checked when the available gauge is at least the target amount, and checking it spends the target amount from the gauge. | Must | Example: gauge 12 → after checking a 6 cell and a 2 cell, 4 remains |
| REQ-040 | SYS | A streak cell is activated when cashback is received on N consecutive days based on the cashback batch date. | Must | The reference date is the cashback batch date (not the Bingo Batch run date); judged when the Bingo Batch runs. No daily amount condition (counted if 0.01 or more is reflected). Retroactive. One streak opens all streak cells with N or lower. If that day's cashback batch has not finished at Bingo Batch time, the day is not judged as a break and is judged at the next Bingo Batch (confirmed 2026-10-02; the cashback system provides per-exchange, per-date completion status) |
| REQ-041 | FR | An activated streak cell is checked without using the gauge. | Must | No gauge change on check |
| REQ-042 | SYS | A streak cell, once activated, is recorded in history and stays active even if the streak breaks. | Must | Checkable even if the streak breaks later |
| REQ-043 | SYS | If the streak breaks before a streak cell opens, the streak count restarts from 0. | Must | Current streak reset to 0; longest streak kept |
| REQ-044 | FR | A checked cell cannot be canceled or moved. | Must | No cancel function |
| REQ-045 | FR | Cells can be checked only during the participation period. | Must | No checking from the aggregation period on |

### 1.5 Rewards · Ranking

| ID | Type | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria |
|----|------|----------|----------|-----------|
| REQ-046 | SYS | Each time one of the 3 rows or 3 columns is completed, a Bingo Reward is given. There are no diagonals. | Must | Line completion time recorded in DB history. No headcount limit |
| REQ-047 | SYS | Checking all 9 cells gives the Full Board Reward. | Must | No headcount limit. Up to 7 rewards per user (6 lines + Full Board) |
| REQ-048 | SYS | There is no reward for checking a single cell. | Must | No per-cell payout |
| REQ-049 | SYS | On ranking ON boards, only Full Board users are ranked by total cashback during the participation period. | Must | Based on total cashback, independent of the amount spent on cells. Updated at each daily Bingo Batch. Completers who withdraw or are reset during the participation period do not leave a vacant place; they drop out of the ranking at the next Bingo Batch recalculation (confirmed 2026-10-12). **Recalculated once right after the aggregation stage starts (the 00:00 UTC stage change on the next day)** to reflect everyone who completed during participation (including users who completed after the last day's Bingo Batch). No new cashback is applied; only the completer list is updated, with the same total cashback and tie rules. The recalculated result is shown on the admin ① Status leaderboard and the user's aggregation-stage leaderboard (confirmed 2026-10-02). The user leaderboard stays frozen at the recalculated result during the aggregation stage, and places vacated in the meantime are applied at announcement; the admin leaderboard shows excluded, withdrawn and forfeited statuses immediately while ranks and total cashback stay fixed (confirmed 2026-10-12). At announcement it is finalized based on this ranking; the aggregation ranking and the final ranking differ only by places vacated after aggregation starts through abuse exclusion, withdrawal or forfeiture (ownership transfer, issue UID designation) (the place is left vacant and lower ranks are not moved up, confirmed 2026-10-02) |
| REQ-050 | SYS | On a ranking tie, the user who completed the board first ranks higher. | Must | Based on the order in which the Full Board record was saved (inserted) into the DB |
| REQ-051 | SYS | The ranking reward pool is run separately from Bingo and Full Board Rewards. | Must | No carry-over between them |
| REQ-052 | FR | The user leaderboard shows the ranks of all winning places and My Rank, with TUIDs masked. | Must | All winning places shown in every stage: participation, aggregation, announcement and payout (e.g., up to 30th if there are 30 winners, confirmed 2026-10-02). Columns are the same in every stage: rank, trader (masked TUID), total cashback, Ranking Reward (REQ-056, confirmed 2026-10-12). If completers are fewer than the winning places, only existing ranks are shown, with no empty rows (e.g., 30 winners and 18 completers = 18 rows). Vacant place rows are not shown and rank numbers are kept (e.g., if 15th is vacant, 16th comes right after 14th, confirmed 2026-10-05). Pagination fixed at 10 rows per page (users cannot change the row count; page numbers + Prev/Next; same in every stage; page buttons hidden if there is only 1 page, confirmed 2026-10-02). My Rank stays fixed below the list regardless of page, and only the My Rank row shows my Ranking Reward amount ("-" if outside the winning places; not added to the My Rewards list, REQ-131). Masking follows the same rule as the Global League (e.g., 99****76). Neither the next update time nor the last updated time is shown; "Updated once a day" (KO "하루 한 번 업데이트돼요") is shown only during participation (confirmed 2026-10-02; last updated time not shown confirmed 2026-10-12). In the aggregation stage, the ranking recalculated right after aggregation starts (all completers during participation) is shown, further updates stop, and "Calculating" (KO "집계 중") is shown with it. Places vacated during the aggregation stage (abuse exclusion, withdrawal, forfeiture) are also left as they are in this stage and applied at announcement (confirmed 2026-10-12). The aggregation ranking and the final ranking differ only by places vacated this way; at announcement those places are left vacant and lower ranks are not moved up (confirmed 2026-10-02). Non-completers see "Complete the Full Board to enter the ranking." (KO "빙고판을 완성하면 랭킹에 참여할 수 있어요") during participation and "You did not complete the Full Board, so you are not ranked." (KO "빙고판을 완성하지 않아 랭킹 대상이 아니에요") after aggregation starts (polite 해요 style, confirmed 2026-10-02). From the announcement stage the final ranking is announced (label "Final Ranking") and stays visible until the payout period ends (404) (no updates after aggregation). Empty state copy: REQ-130. No total such as "All 30 winners" is shown above the leaderboard (the number of rows can differ because of fewer completers or vacant places) (confirmed 2026-10-06) |
